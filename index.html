SAFETY & RESPECT

Kindness is part
of the conversation.

Anonymous spaces still need care. Protect your privacy, respect other people’s boundaries, and think before posting.

✓ Don’t share passwords, addresses, or private identifying details.

✓ Avoid harassment, bullying, threats, and sharing someone else’s private information.

✓ Use reporting or blocking tools where available in the app.

✓ If something feels unsafe, step away and seek help from someone you trust.

Please review the app’s current safety tools and policies. This page does not promise complete anonymity or guarantee that every interaction is moderated.
